Now, one interesting thing.

Section six, one interesting thing.

Tyler Cowen's Marginal Revolution highlights a paper arguing that globalisation's frontier has shifted from factories to offices. The old picture was global value chains in manufacturing, goods crossing borders as parts. The new one is services: accounting, design, support and software work performed across borders. The factory is still larger in gross trade, but the office is where growth is. For a country like Japan, with a shrinking workforce and a deep pool of foreign-facing talent, it is a quietly interesting question which side of that shift it wants to be on. Via Marginal Revolution.
